How the numbers are made

The implied launch value is TVL × exp(w·x) × token odds. ridgeFit in lib/model.ts fits w by ridge regression of log(mcap/tvl) on the snapshot's own figures — TVL, seven-day growth, age without a token, chain count — over every token-bearing protocol, and leaveOneOutFactor and walkForwardFactor test it held out and walk-forward on the same snapshot. tokenOdds takes each category's Laplace-smoothed launch share and oddsAgeFactor lets it fall with age; the scenario range comes from the fit's residual quartiles. farmVerdict prices each plan: capital plus gas against an expected farming share of the implied value, in dollars per hour. Clamps: values are held inside $250,000 to 2% of TVL at the floor and $100T at the ceiling. Every figure is the entry's own claim, derived from the sealed snapshot alone.
